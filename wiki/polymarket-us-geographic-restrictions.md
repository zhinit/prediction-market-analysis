# Polymarket US Geographic Restrictions

Polymarket US is available to verified US users after KYC through the official
iOS app (source: polymarket-us-state-restrictions.md).

## Federal Status

CFTC-regulated Designated Contract Market (DCM). Received Amended Order of
Designation in November 2025. US waitlist removed May 2026; iOS app opened
without invite (source: polymarket-us-state-restrictions.md).

## State-Level Restrictions

### Outright Ban

Minnesota — effective August 1, 2026 (source:
polymarket-us-state-restrictions.md).

### Cease-and-Desist Orders

At least 11 states have issued cease-and-desist orders, including Nevada,
Tennessee, Illinois, Connecticut, and Arizona (source:
polymarket-us-state-restrictions.md).

## Access Requirements

- KYC through official iOS app (ID verification + proof of address)
- API access only available after identity verification
- API keys generated at polymarket.us/developer

(source: polymarket-us-state-restrictions.md, polymarket-us-api-authentication.md)

## International Platform Restrictions

The international Polymarket (polymarket.com) lists the US as close-only
(exit positions only) for both API and frontend. Read endpoints are not
geo-blocked (source: polymarket-international-geoblock.md).
